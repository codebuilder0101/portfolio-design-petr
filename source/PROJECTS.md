# 30 Fresh Portfolio Concepts: Projects 31–60



Thirty new subject-specific briefs, replacing the previous collection’s visual direction with candid photography, stronger crops, direct flash, bold color and practical editing scenarios. These are independent concept studies, not commissioned client engagements.



Each project includes a full-size JPG, a looping GIF, and a silent five-second MP4 at 1280×720, 24 fps. The motion pieces are graphic-design studies: independently animated typography, wipes, reveals, image crops and layout transitions built around AI-generated still artwork. They do not contain filmed action, lip sync or independently animated people/products.



Proposed stacks describe how to produce the full client brief, not tools proven by the generated image. Included still artwork was generated using the built-in image-generation tool; motion previews were assembled programmatically. Do not claim ownership of completed client work or add fabricated results.



No before-and-after proof is supplied for restoration or retouching: a verifiable comparison needs an original source image.
